# 100 Potential Game Ideas — Global Audience Factory Slate (v2)
Date: 2026-10-18. Format per Huzaifa's order: **2-line full idea**, then **vertical slice full detail**. No full-game expansion roadmaps now — those come later.

**Vertical slice design rule (his words):** whatever the full game will contain — all its features, systems, and future possibilities — the player must experience in the slice within **minimum time, space, and place**. Each slice below is designed as a compressed microcosm of its full game: every core system present in miniature, one complete end-to-end run, proving the full game's promise.

Standing rules respected throughout: big feature-rich slices (never basic), instantly understandable with zero nonsense (core mechanic explainable in one line), globally themed for a worldwide mass audience, touch-first mobile + desktop, no gambling, no backend multiplayer (local bots / pass-and-play only).
