# GULLY CHAMPION — Vertical Slice Scope (Batch 1)

**What is playable right now** — one thumb, portrait, touch-first tape-ball cricket.

## Core batting engine
- **Tap-and-hold anywhere** to charge power (live power meter), **release to swing**.
- Release timing is graded: **PERFECT / good / early / late / miss**, with a shrinking
  cue ring at the crease and instant feedback popups.
- **Flick direction**: the last-150ms horizontal flick steers the shot (±58°).
- **Perfect releases** trigger the slow-mo **"GULLY SIX!"** moment: hit-pause, confetti
  cannons, screen shake, crowd roar, umpire call.

## 3 game modes
1. **SUPER OVER** — 6 balls, chase a target set by Tez Tariq.
2. **MOHALLA CUP** — 8-team street bracket (Quarter Final → Semi Final → Final) vs
   bot personalities with distinct bowling styles and banter: Chintu, Chacha Yorker,
   Speedy Shahid, Doosra Daniyal, Bouncer Bilal, Swing Sultan, and final boss
   **Gali Ka Badshah**. Tez Tariq headlines Super Over + Time Attack.
3. **TIME ATTACK** — 60-second endless bowling machine, rising pace, combo multiplier
   (x1–x5) for consecutive boundaries.

## Bowling & rules feel
- **6 delivery types** with readable telegraphs ~0.6s before release (label + hint +
  color glow): yorker, bouncer, slower ball, inswinger, outswinger, doosra.
  Sly bowlers (Badshah) sometimes hide the telegraph.
- **Adaptive bowler AI**: tracks your early/late timing habit and mixes against it.
- Wickets (**bowled** with scattering stumps, **caught** with diving fielders,
  **run-outs** with throw-downs), **wides**, **no-balls** with **free hits**,
  over-by-over scorecard with ball dots.

## World & collection
- **4 gallis**: Rooftop at Sunset, Beach, Mountain Road, Night Street (fairy lights) —
  unlocked via cup progress, each with its own palette, crowd, and ambient life.
- **6 truck-art bat skins** unlocked through career milestones.
- Career stats + records in localStorage; **share card** (downloads a PNG).

## Presentation
- 100% code-drawn HD art: golden-hour gradients, bokeh, dust, ball motion-blur
  trail, squash-and-stretch characters, parallax gallis, animated crowds.
- Fully synthesized WebAudio: bat crack, swelling crowd, umpire calls, per-gali groove.
- Touch-primary + Space/mouse on desktop; full-screen portrait; honest loading gate.
